Games

Free to play, at home or in a session. Some build skills. Some are just for fun.

🍃

Brain Buttons

Help the worried little buddy feel calm with breathing, happy thoughts, a dance party, and the 5 senses.

Calming skills · about 5 minutes 🚦

Stop and Go

Tap on green. Hands off on red. Help the buddy reach the flag.

Practice stopping · 1 minute ✨

Copy Cat

Watch the lights, then copy the pattern. Each round adds one more.

Memory · 1 player 🃏

Friend Match

Flip the cards and find each pair of friends. Play alone or take turns.

Just for fun · 1 or 2 players ⭕

Three in a Row

The classic. Get three in a row. Play a friend, or play the buddy.

Just for fun · 1 or 2 players

These games have no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. Nothing a child does here is saved or sent anywhere. They are for practice and play, and are not therapy or medical advice.
