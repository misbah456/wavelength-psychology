For grown-ups
  • The buddy is the brain’s alarm (the amygdala). Its job is to keep us safe, and sometimes it buzzes when we are safe. The four buttons are ways to tell it “we’re okay.”
  • Practice when your child is calm, so the buttons are ready when feelings get big.
  • In a session: play together on a tablet, or share your screen on a video call and let the child choose. A child can also open this page on their own device.
  • Breathing has a “Just watch” option for when you are sharing your screen.
  • Nothing is saved or sent anywhere. There are no names, accounts, or tracking.
  • This game is for practice and fun. It is not therapy or medical advice.
